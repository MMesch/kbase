# Overnight Oats

No-cook breakfast, prep the night before.

## Ingredients

- 1/2 cup rolled oats
- 1/2 cup milk
- 1/4 cup yogurt
- 1 tbsp chia seeds
- Honey to taste
- Fresh fruit

## Steps

1. Combine oats, milk, yogurt, chia
2. Refrigerate overnight
3. Top with fruit and honey
