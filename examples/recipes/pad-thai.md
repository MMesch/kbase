# Pad Thai

Classic Thai stir-fried noodles.

## Ingredients

- 200g rice noodles
- 2 eggs
- 200g shrimp or tofu
- Bean sprouts
- Tamarind paste
- Fish sauce
- Palm sugar
- Peanuts, crushed

## Steps

1. Soak noodles until pliable
2. Make sauce: tamarind, fish sauce, sugar
3. Stir-fry protein, push aside
4. Scramble eggs
5. Add noodles and sauce
6. Toss with sprouts, top with peanuts
