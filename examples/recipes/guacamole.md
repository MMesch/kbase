# Guacamole

Fresh avocado dip.

## Ingredients

- 3 ripe avocados
- 1 lime, juiced
- 1/2 onion, diced
- Cilantro
- Jalapeño (optional)
- Salt

## Steps

1. Mash avocados to desired texture
2. Mix in lime, onion, cilantro
3. Season with salt
4. Serve immediately with tortilla chips
