# Miso Soup

Simple Japanese soup, perfect for any meal.

## Ingredients

- 4 cups dashi (kombu for vegan)
- 3 tbsp miso paste
- Tofu, cubed
- Wakame seaweed
- Green onions

## Steps

1. Heat dashi, don't boil
2. Dissolve miso in small amount of dashi
3. Add tofu and wakame
4. Stir in miso mixture
5. Serve immediately
