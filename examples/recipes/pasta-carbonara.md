# Pasta Carbonara

Classic Roman pasta with eggs, cheese, and guanciale.

## Ingredients

- 400g spaghetti
- 200g guanciale
- 4 egg yolks
- 100g pecorino romano
- Black pepper

## Steps

1. Cook pasta in salted water
2. Crisp guanciale in pan
3. Mix yolks with cheese
4. Combine off heat

See also [[cacio-e-pepe]] for a simpler cheese pasta.
