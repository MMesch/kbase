# Cacio e Pepe

Simple Roman pasta with cheese and black pepper.

## Ingredients

- 400g tonnarelli or spaghetti
- 200g pecorino romano
- Black pepper

## Steps

1. Cook pasta, reserve water
2. Toast pepper in pan
3. Create emulsion with cheese and pasta water
4. Toss pasta quickly
